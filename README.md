# Ramadan-Aware Demand Forecasting & Inventory Planning for Retail Supply Chains in Tunisia

## Project Overview

This project explores how Ramadan and Eid influence grocery demand in Tunisia and how demand forecasting can support better inventory planning and replenishment decisions in retail supply chains.

Ramadan and Eid are moving holidays whose dates shift approximately 10–11 days earlier each year in the Gregorian calendar. These events can significantly influence grocery purchasing patterns, creating variations in demand across product categories. For example, demand for dates, dairy products and other food categories may increase during specific periods before and throughout Ramadan.

Traditional demand forecasting approaches may struggle to capture these recurring calendar-related effects when religious holidays are not explicitly incorporated into the forecasting process.

To address this challenge, the project develops an end-to-end analytical workflow combining time-series forecasting, inventory planning and business intelligence.

Using a simulated Tunisian grocery sales dataset, we will investigate whether incorporating Ramadan and Eid calendar features improves demand forecasting accuracy and examine how the resulting forecasts can support inventory management and replenishment decisions.

The project also aims to translate analytical results into actionable retail insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**The dataset is simulated and does not represent actual transactions from a Tunisian retailer. Therefore, the project evaluates forecasting and inventory-planning methods under controlled experimental conditions rather than establishing real-world consumer behavior or measured business outcomes.**
